SUJAG EDUCATION - GO LIVE GUIDE (FREE, about 15 minutes)
=========================================================
The website works right away in DEMO MODE (data is stored only in each visitor's own browser).
To make logins, mock-test results, subscriptions, papers and blog posts work online for everyone,
connect a free Firebase project (Spark plan, no credit card needed).

STEP 1 - Create the project
  1. Go to https://console.firebase.google.com and sign in with Google.
  2. Click "Add project", name it (e.g. sujag-education), you can turn Google Analytics off, then Create.

STEP 2 - Turn on login
  1. Build > Authentication > Get started.
  2. Sign-in method tab: enable "Email/Password" and enable "Google" (choose a support email).
  3. Settings tab > Authorized domains: add your website domain (for GitHub Pages: yourname.github.io).

STEP 3 - Create the database
  1. Build > Firestore Database > Create database.
  2. Choose a location near you, start in PRODUCTION mode.
  3. Open the "Rules" tab, delete everything, paste the contents of firestore.rules (included in this folder).
     Replace YOUR_ADMIN_EMAIL@gmail.com with YOUR admin Gmail address. Click Publish.
     IMPORTANT: sign in as admin with "Continue with Google" (the rules require a verified email).

STEP 4 - Connect the website
  1. Project settings (gear icon) > General > "Your apps" > click the Web icon (</>) > register the app.
  2. Copy the firebaseConfig values.
  3. Open sujag-config.js and paste them into FIREBASE: { ... } (remove the comment markers).
  4. In the same file set ADMIN_EMAILS: ["your-admin-email@gmail.com"] (same email as in the rules).

STEP 5 - Upload the website
  Upload ALL files of this folder to your hosting root (GitHub Pages: repository root).
  Every .html file, plus sujag-config.js.

STEP 6 - First login as admin
  1. Open yoursite/login.html and choose "Continue with Google" using your admin Gmail.
  2. Open yoursite/admin.html.
  3. Settings > "Import starter tests & posts" to add 5 ready-made mock tests and 2 blog posts.
  4. Settings > set your plan prices and your JazzCash / Easypaisa / bank account details (shown on the Pricing page).
  5. Add past papers (paste Google Drive links), blog posts and your own mock tests from the tabs.
     Tick "Free" on the few tests/papers you want everyone with an account to try; the rest are Premium.

HOW PAYMENTS & PREMIUM WORK
  1. Student picks a plan on the Pricing page, pays you by JazzCash / Easypaisa / bank, and submits the transaction ID.
  2. Admin Panel > Subscriptions shows the pending request. Check your account, then click Approve.
  3. Premium is activated for the plan length (renewals add time on top). Reject or Revoke any time.
  4. You can also grant Premium manually by email (for WhatsApp / cash payments).
  Premium questions and paper links live in separate protected documents; Firestore rules only release them
  to Premium members, so students cannot read them without a subscription.
  NOTE: a Google Drive link that you share as "Anyone with the link" can be forwarded by a paying member.
  Past papers shown as Preview/Download are only as private as that Drive link. Mock tests are fully protected.

HOW STUDENT DATA REACHES YOU
  When a student takes a mock test, the score, answers and time are saved to Firebase.
  Admin Panel > Results shows every attempt; Admin Panel > Users shows every registered student.
  Both can be exported to CSV (opens in Excel).

PAST PAPERS WITH GOOGLE DRIVE
  Upload the PDF to Google Drive > Share > "Anyone with the link" > Viewer > Copy link.
  Admin Panel > Past Papers > Add > paste the link. Premium students get Preview and Download buttons.
  Folder links also work (they open in Drive).

BULK-ADDING QUESTIONS
  Admin Panel > Mock Tests > Add (or Edit) > "Bulk import". Paste questions like:
     Q: What is the capital of Pakistan?
     A) Karachi
     B) Lahore
     C) Islamabad
     D) Peshawar
     Answer: C
     Explain: Islamabad is the capital.
  (Leave a blank line between questions.)

FREE LIMITS (Spark plan, generous for a start)
  Firestore: 50,000 reads and 20,000 writes per day. Authentication: free for email and Google sign-in.

DEMO MODE (before Firebase)
  Demo admin login: admin@sujag.demo / admin123 (works only in demo mode, only in your browser).
  Demo jobs and papers are samples. They disappear once Firebase is connected.

SECURITY NOTES
  - The Firebase apiKey in sujag-config.js is meant to be public. The rules protect your data.
  - Only emails listed in the rules can change content or see all users and results.
  - Never share your admin Google account.
